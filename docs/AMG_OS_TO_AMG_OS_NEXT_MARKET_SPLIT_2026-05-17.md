# AMG OS To AMG OS NEXT Market Split

Date: 2026-05-17

## Rule

`AMG_OS` is the live internal workflow lane.

`AMG_OS_NEXT` is the later market/productization lane.

## AMG_OS Focus

The immediate work is for AMG and Amy:

- keyframe and cover selection
- delivery preparation
- spreadsheet-to-deliverable tooling
- translation workflow
- review/proof logs
- internal workflow cleanup
- payroll/tool waste reduction

## AMG_OS_NEXT Focus

The later product is a superior competitor to TubeSiteSubmitter,
TubeSubmitter, NoxoVision, and related adult submission/backend workflow tools.

Competitor research and SaaS positioning belong in `AMG_OS_NEXT` after
internal AMG workflows prove they save time, reduce errors, and improve proof
of completion.

## Promotion Rule

Do not promote a feature from `AMG_OS` into `AMG_OS_NEXT` because it sounds
marketable.

Promote it only after the internal workflow proves real value for AMG/Amy.
